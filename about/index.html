How We Started

Born from a need we couldn't look away from

Our organisation was born out of a deeply felt need to support the most vulnerable families in Uttar Pradesh. Seeing children miss out on basic schooling due to poverty, and families struggling for food or facing sudden crises without a safety net, inspired us to step in.

We started this journey in Prayagraj to bridge these gaps — ensuring that no child is left behind, and that emergency help is always within reach for those who need it most. What began as a response to families on the ghats has grown into a set of programs reaching 12+ districts across Uttar Pradesh.

— Ashish Singh, Founder

Mission, Vision & Values

What guides everything we do

🎯 Mission

Why we exist

We exist to uplift underprivileged children and families in the Prayagraj area of Uttar Pradesh by providing access to quality education, critical food support, and timely emergency assistance.

🌅 Vision

The world we want

A world where every child has the opportunity to learn, no family suffers from hunger, and vulnerable communities are supported during emergencies.

Our Approach

Change starts at home, and works outward

We don't believe in handing out aid and walking away. We work at every level that shapes a child's life — starting with the family and reaching all the way to the systems around them.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family

Helping parents understand and act on their children's developmental, educational and health needs.

🏘️

Community

Mobilising volunteers and local leaders to take collective responsibility for children's wellbeing.

🏛️

Public Systems & Partners

Working alongside schools, local administration, and CSR partners such as PCPL Foundation and Sampoorna Shiksha to extend our reach.
