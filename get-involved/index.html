Give safely, in a few taps

Every donation is processed securely through Razorpay - India's trusted payment gateway - so your money reaches us instantly, with an automatic receipt for your records.

Donate via Razorpay

Pay by card, UPI, netbanking or wallet. The form collects your name, PAN and address for your 80G tax receipt, along with the donation amount.

Having trouble paying online? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you through it.
